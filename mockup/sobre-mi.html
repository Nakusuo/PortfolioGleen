hi, i'm glenn

mixed media artist & animator

Glenn (she/they) is a visual artist from Peru. She prints photographs and works over them by hand with oil, acrylic, glitter and rhinestones — then scans and animates them.

Her work has accompanied Hilary Duff's Lucky Me Tour announcement, sombr's sold-out show at Honda Center and twenty one pilots' Ohio Stadium show. She also developed animation tests for Ella by Hera Roja Films.
